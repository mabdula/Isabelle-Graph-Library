HOW ONE MEASURED RUN WAS LAUNCHED -- hybrid bipartite matching solver
====================================================================

Every number in Tables 35 to 37 of the paper derives from runs launched as
follows (campaign 20261016_003216, 810 runs, all OPTIMAL with
the oracle's certificate accepted; its raw rows and its conditions file are in
published_results/hybrid_timings.zip).

THE PROBLEM
  One DIMACS .asn instance (instances/README.txt).  Families low-cost and
  two-cost at n = 2048, 4096, 8192, 16384, 32768 (the study grid) and
  65536, 131072, 262144, 524288, 1048576 (added for this solver); geometric at
  n = 256 .. 4096 and 8192, 16384.  Generator seeds 1..10 at every size.

THE COMMAND
  taskset -c <core> timeout -k 10 1324 python3 ../run/warm_then_exec.py 3 -- \
      python3 code/driver/asn_solve.py --binary code/driver/asn_solver_bench \
      --timeout 1200 --csv --tag "<family>;rep=<k>;warmcore=3s" <instance.asn>
  which is, with the launcher of this package,
  taskset -c <core> timeout -k 10 1324 python3 ../run/warm_then_exec.py 3 -- \
      ./bench_hybrid --timeout 1200 --tag "<family>;rep=<k>;warmcore=3s" <instance.asn>
  (the outer timeout is 1200 + 120 + 3 + 1 s; the solver's own cap, enforced
  by asn_solve.py, is 1200 s of wall time).

THE WARM SPIN
  warm_then_exec.py (run/ at the top of evaluation/) narrows the affinity to the one core it is on, busy-spins
  for 3 s and then execs the solver on that core.  The spin is a separate
  process and is outside every clock the solver reads.  Every published row
  carries warmcore=3s in its tag.

REPEATS AND AGGREGATION
  Three repeats per instance, EACH ITS OWN PROCESS.  Per instance, the
  repeat whose cpu_ms is the median of the three; per (family, size) the mean
  over the ten instances of that per-instance value, in milliseconds in the
  paper (Oracle, Cert., Check, Glue, Total; Total/Oracle is the ratio of the
  two means).  See code/OUTPUT.txt for the mapping of the columns.

PINNING
  Seven runs at a time, one per L3 island (cores 4, 8, ..., 28 of the eight
  4-core islands), cores 0-3 reserved for everything that is not a
  measurement.  Families run one after another, sizes in ascending order.

TIMEOUT AND LADDER
  1200 s per run.  A run that reaches it is recorded TIMEOUT with empty time
  columns; the other repeats of that instance and all larger sizes of the
  family are recorded TIMEOUT_INFERRED without being run.  No run of the
  published campaign timed out.

THE BINARY
  code/driver/asn_solver_bench as built by `make' here: MLton 20241230 with
  -default-ann 'allowFFI true' -default-type int64 -inline 10000 -link-opt -lm,
  C parts with gcc 11.4.0 at -O2 (CSA at -O3 -std=gnu89, csa_q variant).
  The binary that ran had md5 aadaec393930d9ccf1eaec97b01a7532; the export it
  was compiled from has md5 232e15a004db03c56b5233646e8bc132 (make check).

INSTANCE FILES OF THE ADDED SIZES
  No checksum was recorded for the files of the added sizes (low-cost/two-cost
  n >= 65536, geometric n >= 8192); they are regenerated from their seeds with
  instances/generators/ (see instances/README.txt).

THE CLOCK
  CLOCK_PROCESS_CPUTIME_ID, compiled into the solver; cpu_ms = t_done -
  t_parsed is the verified program and nothing else; reading the file is
  before the first stamp and writing the solution after the last.
